# Decision log

Transient working ledger. `cg-unblock` writes entries; every `cg-sign-off` reconciles resolved entries in its scope, even without a declared harvest phase. Preserve actual authority and dispositions in completion evidence, consolidate enduring meaning into project context or its contract/catalog owner, then drain consumed entries. Keep pending questions and entries still needed by unfinished work. A permanent
contract may never cite this file or a plan ticket ID as the authority for a rule, and `cg verify`
fails the build when one does.

Numbering is stable and never reused. `DA-NN` (autonomous) goes straight to *Resolved*. `DU-NN`
(owner) starts in *Pending your review* and moves here once answered — do not duplicate or
renumber. Entry shape lives in `.agents/skills/cg-unblock/assets/decision-entry.template.md`.

Highest allocated IDs: DA-00, DU-00. Advance these counters when assigning IDs; retain them when entries are drained.
